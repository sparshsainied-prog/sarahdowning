Flesh and Blood

A murdered family. One survivor. A vampire with amnesia.

Vampire Richard Dawes wakes beside the murdered bodies of a respected vampirologist and his family, with no memory of the night before.

If he is to figure out the truth, Richard is going to have to work with the one person who has every reason to hate him…

Samantha Woods returns home to find her family slaughtered and the household wards intact. With no signs of tampering, the police are quick to blame her father. As the papers call for a national cull, Samantha is determined to clear his name.

As Richard’s memories begin to return, it becomes clear that what happened that night wasn’t an accident.

The Wulfharan Series

A character-driven dark fantasy in four books, set in a world where gods walk among mortals, magic is part of everyday life, and shapeshifters are bound to an ancient and troubled past. It begins when Coiran, who has known nothing but slavery, is rescued by Elam, a disowned prince.

Sarah Downing

I spent a decade studying Traditional English Martial Arts in order to write better fight scenes.

I write character-driven dark fantasy about human bonds formed in adversity, shaped by folklore, ecological realism and history. Away from my desk I’m watching wildlife, or adding to an ever-growing hoard of books.
